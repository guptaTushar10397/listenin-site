ListenIn Privacy Policy

Your privacy, in plain words.

ListenIn is built to share music, not your personal information.

Last updated 17 November 2026

Privacy at a glance

  • No account needed

    There's nothing to sign up for. No email or password needed.

  • No ads, no tracking

    ListenIn never tracks you across other apps or websites, and we never sell your information.

  • Your room sees your name

    People in your room see your display name and the songs being played, queued and requested.

  • Rooms don't last

    A room is deleted when the host ends it, and any room left open closes within a day.

  • Anonymous, never linked to you

    A little anonymous information helps us see which features people use and fix crashes.

  • Your music stays yours

    Music plays from your own Apple Music. ListenIn never sends or keeps any audio.

The full policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information ListenIn (“we”) uses, why we use it, who helps us and how long we keep it. It applies to the ListenIn app for iPhone, iPad and iPhone Duo. ListenIn has no accounts and no ads. We use information only to run your listening rooms, to fix crashes and improve the app, to let you know when an update is available, and to reply if you email us.

No account needed

You don't need an account to use ListenIn. We never ask for your real name, email, phone number, Apple Account or password. When you first open the app, you get a random guest name. You can change it any time in Profile. People in the rooms you join see this name. So rooms know who's who, ListenIn gives your device an anonymous ID. It isn't linked to you and is never used to track you.

What a room keeps, and who sees it

While a room is open, we keep:

  • the room code, when the room was started, and its settings, such as whether new people are let in automatically
  • who's in the room, by display name, and when they joined
  • requests to join, with the person's display name, while the host decides
  • what's playing: the song, artist and cover art, and how far into the song it is, so everyone hears the same moment
  • the songs in the Up Next queue and song requests, with the display name of the person who added or asked for each one

Only the host and the people in a room can see what's in it. If you ask to join, you see only your own request until the host lets you in. Everyone in a room sees each other's names and the songs, so choose a name you're happy to share.

Songs can only be picked from Apple Music. There's no chat, and you can't upload photos or files. Your avatar color is picked automatically, and others in the room see it next to your name.

Improving the app

ListenIn uses a little anonymous information to see which features people use and to find and fix crashes and slowdowns. It's never linked to you, never includes your name, and is never used for tracking or advertising.

  • How the app is used: for example, which screens you open and when you start or join a room, plus your approximate location (your country or region), worked out from your internet connection. Never your precise location.
  • Crashes: what went wrong if the app closes unexpectedly, so we can fix it.
  • Speed: how quickly the app opens and loads, so we can make it faster.

This includes things like opening the app, starting, joining or leaving a room, sharing a room code, and playing, requesting or approving songs. It may include the room code and which song was played, but never your name. Room codes are random and aren't linked to you.

No ads, no tracking

ListenIn has no ads. It doesn't track you across other companies' apps and websites, so it never asks for permission to track you. We never sell your information.

We don't collect your precise location, contacts, financial or health information, purchases, browsing history, Apple Account, or email address (unless you email us).

Apple Music

Music plays through Apple Music on each person's own device, using their own subscription. ListenIn never streams, uploads or stores music itself, and never sees your Apple Account or your music library. If you browse your own library, it stays on your device. Only songs you play or add in a room are shared with that room.

On your device

Your display name and a few app settings are saved on your device. While you're in a room, or waiting to join one, your name is shared with that room. Deleting ListenIn removes your name and settings from your device.

When you open ListenIn, it checks with our service whether a newer version is available so it can let you know. This check never includes your name.

How long we keep information

When you leave a room, you're removed from it. Songs you added or requested stay in the room's queue, with your name, until the room is deleted. A request to join disappears once the host answers, if you cancel it, or after a short time with no answer. When the host ends a room, everything in it is deleted. Any room left open closes within a day and can no longer be joined. Anonymous usage and crash information is deleted automatically after a limited time.

Who helps us run ListenIn

We use a small number of trusted service providers, such as Google and Apple, to run rooms, connect to Apple Music, send us crash reports and deliver music. They handle information only on our behalf. Music comes from Apple Music. We never sell your information or use it for advertising or tracking.

Children

ListenIn is rated 4+ but isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. The only thing people can write in ListenIn is their display name.

Your choices and rights

You can keep your guest name or change it any time, leave any room, and delete the app to remove your name and settings from your device.

We use room information to run the rooms you start or join. We use anonymous usage and crash information because it's needed to keep ListenIn working well for everyone. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see, correct or delete your information, or to object to how it's used. To ask, email support.listenin@gmail.com. Since there's no account, it helps to include your room code if your question is about a room. The companies that help us run ListenIn may handle information in the United States and other countries.

Changes to this policy

If the way ListenIn uses information changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email us at support.listenin@gmail.com.

You can email us any time from your own mail app. If you email us from Profile in the app, the email includes a few details, such as your app version, your iOS or iPadOS version and whether you're on iPhone or iPad, to help us help you. You can see and change these before you send it. We use your email only to reply to you.
