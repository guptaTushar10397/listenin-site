ListenIn

We don't stream music. We synchronize experiences.

A real-time social listening app for iPhone and iPad. Start a room, share a short code with friends, and everyone hears the same song at the same moment — each playing through their own Apple Music.

Powered by Apple Music Real-time sync No account

How ListenIn works

🎶

Start a room

Pick a song and you're hosting. ListenIn gives you a short code to share with friends.

👥

Invite friends

Anyone with the code joins your room in a tap — no sign-up, no password.

📡

Listen in sync

Play, pause, queue, and skip as the host. Everyone stays perfectly in time.

Private by design

No sign-up and no profile — ListenIn connects you anonymously. A room shares only the display name you choose and the songs being played; the music itself plays through your own Apple Music, on your own device. No analytics, no ads, no tracking.
