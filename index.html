Listen together.
Same song, same second.

Start a room from any song on Apple Music, share a six-character code, and everyone hears it at the same moment, each on their own device.

How it works

Three steps to a room.

  1. 1

    Pick a song.

    Browse charts and playlists or search Apple Music. Tap any song and your room is live.

  2. 2

    Share the code.

    Every room gets a six-character code. Copy it, or tap Share to send an invite link.

  3. 3

    Let friends in.

    Friends ask to join with your code, and you let them in. Or turn on Auto-approve, and anyone with the code joins straight away.

Codes leave out look-alike characters such as 0 and O, or 1 and I, so they're easy to read out loud.

You're the host

You decide
who comes in.

Friends ask to join with your code, and their request waits for you. Let them in, or decline. Want it open? Turn on Auto-approve joins, and anyone with the code joins instantly. It's off until you turn it on, so no one gets in without your OK.

Always in step

Everyone hears
the same moment.

The host sets the pace: play, pause, skip. Everyone in the room follows. ListenIn keeps everyone in step with the host and quietly catches up anyone who slips. If you fall behind, a Sync button appears in your player. One tap brings you back.3

Privacy

Private by design.

No sign-up, no email, no password. Just open the app and you're in. ListenIn gives you a guest name, which you can change any time.

  • No ads, no tracking. ListenIn shows no ads and never tracks you across other apps or websites.
  • A room shares only what it needs. The people in your room see your display name and the songs being played, queued and requested.
  • Rooms don't last. When the host ends a room, it's deleted. And every room ends on its own within a day.
  • A little anonymous information, never linked to you. ListenIn uses it to see which features people use, fix crashes and keep the app running smoothly. It's never used to track you.
  • Your music stays yours. ListenIn never sends or keeps any audio. Your music plays from your own Apple Music.

FAQ

Questions? Answers.

Do my friends need Apple Music too?

Yes. Each person plays the music through their own Apple Music subscription on their own device. ListenIn only keeps everyone on the same song at the same spot.

Why am I waiting to join?

The host lets each person in. If they've turned on Auto-approve joins, you're in straight away. If the host doesn't answer after a little while, your request ends and you can ask again.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes, and on iPhone Duo. On iPad and on an unfolded iPhone Duo, the room and the queue show side by side.

How long does a room last?

Until the host ends it, and never longer than a day. If the host has been away for a while, the room stops letting new people in.

What if I'm already in a room?

Opening another invite asks whether you want to switch rooms first. Confirm, and ListenIn leaves your current room and asks to join the new one.
