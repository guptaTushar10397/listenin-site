Getting started

What is ListenIn?

A way to listen to Apple Music with friends. One person starts a room and shares a code. The music plays from each person's own Apple Music, and ListenIn keeps everyone on the same song at the same moment.

Is ListenIn free?

Yes, with no in-app purchases. Everyone who starts or joins a room needs their own active Apple Music subscription. Browsing songs and charts is free.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up, email or password. Just open the app and you get a guest name, which you can change any time.

Which devices does it work on?

iPhone, iPad and iPhone Duo with iOS or iPadOS 26 or later. On a bigger screen you can see the room and the queue together. There's no Mac or Apple Watch app.

Rooms & friends

How do I start a room and invite friends?

Open the Music tab and tap any song. Your room starts and you get a six-character code. On the Room tab, tap Copy or Share to send it to friends.

How do I join a room?

Open the Room tab, enter the code your friend shared, and tap Join Room. Once the host lets you in, you're listening together.

Why can't I get in?

The host needs to let you in. If the host doesn't answer after a little while, your request ends and you can ask again. If the code doesn't work, check all six characters, or the room may have ended.

How long does a room last?

Until the host ends it, or for up to a day. If the host has been away for a while, new people can't join.

Apple Music & playback

What if I fall out of sync?

Everyone's internet connection is a little different, and ListenIn fixes small gaps on its own. If you fall behind, or you're paused while the host is playing, a Sync button appears in the player. Tap it to catch up with everyone.

How do I request a song?

As a listener, open the queue, tap Request a Song, and pick a song from Apple Music. The host approves or declines each request.

Music won't play

Check that Apple Music is turned on for ListenIn in Settings > ListenIn, that your Apple Music subscription is active, and that you're online. Then tap Sync, or leave and rejoin the room.

Privacy & account

How do I change my name?

Go to Profile > Edit Name. People in your rooms see this name. Leave it empty to use your guest name.

Does ListenIn track me?

No. There are no ads, and ListenIn doesn't track you across other apps or websites. We use a little anonymous information to see which features people use and to fix crashes. It's never linked to you. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data?

When you leave a room, you're removed from it, and when the host ends a room, everything in it is deleted. Every room closes within a day. Deleting the app removes your name and settings from your device. For anything else, email support.listenin@gmail.com.

Still need help?

Email us and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

Email support

We usually reply within a few days.

Tip: in the app, tap Profile > Report an Issue. It adds a few details about your device so we can help faster.
